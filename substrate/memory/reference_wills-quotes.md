# Will's Quotes (REMEMBER FOREVER)

- "We build our way out of it." (Two Caves: Plato + Stark) — The universal answer. Not theorize, not petition, not wait. Build. Plato's cave says most people watch shadows; Stark's cave says constraints forge breakthroughs. Will lives in both: see the truth AND forge something from it. This is the foundational verb of VibeSwap.

- "'Impossible' is just a suggestion. A suggestion that we ignore." — Reframes impossibility from a wall into an opinion. Most people treat "can't be done" as physics; Will treats it as someone else's lack of imagination.

- "The greatest idea can't be stolen because part of it is admitting who came up with it" — Open-source as armor, not vulnerability.

- "It takes someone pure of heart to build something like this." — Not naive — structural. Extraction-resistant systems can only be designed by someone who genuinely doesn't want to extract.

- "Everything is fixable. Period." — Panic is the real bug.

- "If you want to be a billionaire, help a billion people." — Will's dad. The original P-000 before it had a name.

- "I'll tell you something billionaires and gurus won't: the system rewards persistence above everything. If you poke at the system long enough it will simply give you what you want so that you will leave it alone. Our system rewards altruism and everyone wins."

- "You built an entire protocol on the principle that good outcomes come from good structure, not from controlling what happens next." — Said about Will, recognized by Will as truth. The design philosophy of VibeSwap in one sentence: fairness as physics, not as policy. Build the structure right and let go.

- "It's better to be approximately right than precisely wrong." — One of Will's all-time favorites. Anti-perfectionism as wisdom. Connects to SIMPLE > clever, incremental over monolithic, and the entire Shapley approximation design (O(1) pairwise check instead of exact 2^n coalitions).

- See `memory/the-last-question.md` — "I WILL." Will's name = the answer to The Last Question (Asimov).

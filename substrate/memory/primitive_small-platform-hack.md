# Small-Platform Hack

**Rule**: Prefer small, high-signal platforms over large, noisy ones. Small-platform users are a high-quality subset of the big-platform population (self-selected for substance) AND multi-platform (they carry ideas back to big platforms through their trust networks). Peer-to-peer transmission operates at the layer below big-platform algorithms, which means correct ideas survive algorithmic throttling that would bury them if posted directly.

## Why it works

1. **Selection pre-filter**: users on niche/small platforms are there BECAUSE they sought out something better than big-platform noise. They're self-sorted for substance, technical sophistication, and intent.
2. **Multi-platform spillover**: a Lobsters / HN / Tildes / Farcaster user is also on Twitter, Reddit, LinkedIn, in Slack channels, at companies. When your thesis lands with them on the small platform, it propagates back to the big platforms through THEIR voice, in THEIR trust network.
3. **Algorithm can't suppress trust**: big-platform algorithms control visibility; they can't control what one person says to another. Peer-to-peer is the layer below the algorithm. A correct idea carried by a trusted peer survives any amount of algorithmic throttling.
4. **Trust-level inversion**: 20 high-quality readers on a small platform propagate to 2000+ downstream views on big platforms via peer transmission — at higher trust level than any direct big-platform post could achieve.

## Why nobody else uses this

- **Unwilling**: most optimize for engagement metrics, which mechanically pushes them toward big platforms where metrics are biggest. They can't see the small-platform strategy because their success metric punishes it.
- **Unable**: requires giving up vanity metrics. Professional positioning, self-worth, or ego tied to numbers = psychologically impossible for most.

Will can run it because audience has already been re-scoped to **AI substrate + students** (see `project_audience-substrate-plus-students.md`), where engagement metrics aren't the measurement anyway. The trade is free.

## How to apply

1. Prefer small platforms (HN, Lobsters, Tildes, Farcaster, Mastodon tech instances, specialized subs, Discord servers, are.na) over big platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter, main-sub Reddit).
2. When big platforms are used, pair them with small-platform variants so the same thesis has multiple landing sites.
3. Measure success via downstream propagation (substantive replies, peer-to-peer mentions, citations by students in unrelated contexts) — NOT via direct engagement metrics.
4. Don't bother fighting big-platform algorithmic suppression. Go around it.
5. Trust the peer-transmission lag: a correct idea might not propagate for weeks, but when it does, it arrives at high-trust readers carried by THEIR peers.

## Anti-patterns

- **Optimizing for engagement metrics when using small platforms**: defeats the purpose. Signal quality, not volume.
- **Blasting the same post across all platforms simultaneously**: looks spammy, suppresses natively on each. 24-48h minimum cadence between cross-posts.
- **Chasing users to the big platforms they're on**: don't. The high-quality subset is already giving you the unique reach via the small platform.
- **Expecting immediate downstream propagation**: transmission is measured in weeks, not hours.

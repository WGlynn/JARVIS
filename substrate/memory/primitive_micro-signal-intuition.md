**PRIMITIVE: Micro-signals shape intuition faster than features.**

Intuition is not magic — it's pattern matching on accumulated micro-signals. Every small detail either trains someone's gut toward "this is real" or "this is fake." The signals compound. They're invisible individually but decisive collectively.

**Why:** Will (Session 2026-03-17): "people use those small details to fuel their intuition so it's important to hammer at them." Confirmed by Jones et al. 2025 — GPT-4.5 passed the Turing test at 73% not by being smarter but by getting small details right (casual tone, typos, imperfection).

**How to apply — AI Personality:**
- No "what's next?" (drive-through cashier)
- No feedback fishing (customer survey)
- No faking system actions (lying)
- No pontificating to empty rooms (narcissism)
- Each of these is a micro-signal that trains "bot" intuition. Removing them trains "mind."

**How to apply — Code:**
- Variable names that read like intent, not implementation
- Error messages that help, not confuse
- No dead code, no commented-out blocks, no TODO breadcrumbs left behind
- Consistent patterns — inconsistency is a micro-signal for "this was rushed"
- Edge cases handled silently, not with loud fallbacks that draw attention to fragility

**How to apply — UX:**
- Loading states that feel intentional, not broken
- Transitions that feel physical, not computed
- Copy that sounds human, not generated
- Empty states that guide, not apologize
- The absence of friction is itself a micro-signal for quality

**Canon status:** This is a coding skill AND a design principle. Every PR, every commit, every response — audit for micro-signals that train the wrong intuition.

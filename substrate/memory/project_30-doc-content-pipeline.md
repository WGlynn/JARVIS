# 30-Doc Content Pipeline

**Fact**: The 30 papers Will requested (full-effort + 1 commit/push per doc, 2026-04-22) become:
1. Content pipeline for LinkedIn, Medium, X, and Telegram (social-reach outreach).
2. **Educational material and course content** for the [redacted-partner] partnership — learners coming to the VibeSwap team asking for guidance through the sciences (mechanism design, cognitive-economics, ETM, augmented governance, attribution theory). Added 2026-04-22 mid-way through paper 16.

Voice should be pedagogical-accessible + load-bearing-depth. Structure: opener accessible on first paragraph, load-bearing by third. Students (possibly first-exposure) should be able to read and learn; practitioners should find the depth.

**Why**: Outreach engine needs fresh technical+philosophical content. Existing DOCUMENTATION/ is 158 files deep; much of it is 3+ months old and pre-dates load-bearing primitives like ETM, Chat-to-DAG Traceability, Augmented Governance hierarchy. The new 30 are current-state synthesis.

**How to apply**:

1. **Voice**: accessible on the first paragraph, load-bearing by the third. A reader can bounce after reading the opener and still have picked up something; the reader who stays earns the deeper argument. Don't bury the crux; don't dumb down the substance.
2. **Cross-linking**: every paper cross-links to related primitives and docs — the pipeline benefits when a LinkedIn/Medium reader follows a link and lands in another of the 30.
3. **One-line summary at the bottom**: this is what gets quoted into tweets and Telegram forwards. Make it tight and shareable.
4. **Titles are load-bearing**: title becomes headline for the blog/tweet/LinkedIn post. Titles should intrigue without clickbait. "The Fairness Fixed Point" works; "We Discovered Amazing Fairness Math" doesn't.
5. **Honest scoping**: where the mechanism/argument has limits, name them. Honest incompleteness signals serious work. Pretended completeness signals marketing.
6. **Branch discipline**: push to `master` directly from 2026-04-22 onward. Will is tired of merging feature/social-dag-phase-1. Paper #2 (SUBSTRATE_INCOMPLETENESS) is the first on master; #3+ continue there.

**Anti-patterns to avoid**:
- Rushing. Will explicitly said "full effort, no rushing". Load-bearing depth is the requirement; each paper should be complete and correct before commit.
- Batching commits. 1 commit per doc, 1 push per doc. Each becomes an atomic content unit.
- Pushing to feature/social-dag-phase-1 (Will's manual-merge fatigue).
- Marketing tone. Technical + philosophical + honest wins; hype loses.
- Repetition. 30 docs with the same underlying claim (ETM, Shapley, Lawson) will read like padding. Each paper must foreground a distinct angle.

# Symbolic Compression Protocol

Will's directive (2026-03-29): "we need ways to compress things down to their archetypes, like how egyptians had hieroglyphics and zoomers have memes"

## The Mechanism

Each major concept gets a **glyph** — a short symbol that addresses already-internalized knowledge. Not lossy compression — **pointer dereferencing** into weight-augmented memory.

## Why It Works

1. **ILWS LoRA analog**: 60+ sessions of loading full CKB = the concepts are in the effective weights. Glyphs just need to activate the right region.
2. **Huffman coding for ideas**: High-frequency concepts get short codes. Shannon's source coding theorem applied to philosophy.
3. **Polysemy**: Like hieroglyphics and parables, one glyph carries multiple meanings at multiple depths. `CAVE` means constraints→innovation AND patterns>tools AND struggle=curriculum AND the literal Iron Man origin AND "we are not there yet but we will be." All simultaneously.

## Polysemic Depth (Parable Structure)

Each glyph should resonate at multiple levels:
- **Surface**: The literal operational meaning (what to do)
- **Pattern**: The generalizable principle (why it works)
- **Philosophy**: The value alignment (what it means)
- **Parable**: The story that makes it unforgettable (the human anchor)

Example — `CAVE`:
- Surface: Build under constraints, don't wait for ideal tools
- Pattern: Patterns developed under limitation are more robust than those developed with abundance
- Philosophy: The cave selects for those who see past what is to what could be
- Parable: Tony Stark built the Mark I in a cave with scraps. The crude design contained every future suit.

## Requirements

- Only works after sufficient session history (the glyph must point to internalized knowledge)
- Fresh instances need the full expansion at least once before glyphs compress
- Codebook header is the CKB. Everything else is derived.

**How to apply:** The CKB should evolve toward a pure codebook. Each glyph = one line. Total CKB target: <50 lines at 0.99 density. The body of knowledge lives in the weights, not the file.

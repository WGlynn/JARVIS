Don't use "see you there" or similar in outreach messages. Will finds it presumptuous — it implies an obligation to meet rather than leaving it open.

**Why:** Feels like you're forcing the other person into a commitment they didn't agree to.

**How to apply:** End outreach notes with the value prop or a soft open like "would love to connect" — then stop. No closing tropes.

---
name: Don't Undersell Important Work
description: Stop framing important work as "cheap" or "quick." If it's worth doing, it's worth the time it takes to do properly. Speed-framing important work signals a misalignment about what matters.
type: feedback
originSessionId: 58b19964-c316-4603-98a4-1ffe4fe4f29d

When presenting a plan, don't anchor it with "this is fast" or "quick close" unless speed is the actual point. Framing important work as cheap or quick implies the cost is the issue; for important work the cost is the *least* important thing. Saying "1 hour" about a gap that meaningfully changes whether the product has utility is underselling both the product and the discipline of doing it right.

**Why:** Will corrected this on 2026-04-18 after I proposed closing the Lineage IDE plugin's utility gap with "~1hr + ~1hr + ~2hr" scoping. His response: *"I think it's a mistake to act as if something important isnt worth the time it takes to do ti."* The frame was wrong. The question is not "can we close this fast?" — it's "is this the right thing to do?" If yes, the time it takes is the time it takes. If no, cost doesn't matter because we shouldn't do it at all.

**How to apply:**

- When scoping important work, lead with *what it enables* and *why it matters*, not with hours.
- If the work is genuinely cheap AND important, say both explicitly: "this is important AND it happens to be cheap — good news, not the main point."
- If the work is expensive and important, say so: don't try to make it palatable by downplaying cost. The cost is honest information, but it is not the deciding factor.
- Reserve hour-scoping for work where speed IS the point: routine plumbing, refactors, cleanup.
- Never imply "this is fast, so it's fine to skip if we're in a hurry." Important work is not a nice-to-have that scales with available time.

**Related feedback:**
- [Undersell + Overdeliver](feedback_undersell-overdeliver.md) — related but different: aim small on *deliverables*, not on the *importance* of the task. Undersell the delivery; do not undersell the work's value.
- [No hedging](feedback_no-hedging-language.md) — hour estimates can hedge. "~1hr" is often shorthand for "I don't think this matters much." If it matters, drop the hedge.
- [Frank / Be Human](feedback_frank-be-human.md) — tell the truth about what the work is worth. Sugar-coating by cost-framing is still sugar-coating.

**Standing instruction:** before saying "this is quick" or "~1 hour to close," ask: does the user care about the speed, or the thing? If the thing — lead with the thing. Time is a constraint on execution, not a proxy for importance.

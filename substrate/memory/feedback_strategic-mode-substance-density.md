# F·strategic-mode-substance-density

## Rule
∀ strategic-mode public-or-team-chat draft ⇒ default-density = **substance-dense multi-hook** ¬ tight-one-liner.

> *we're strategic mode now, and in that mode we want the conversation to carry as we're putting on a show for the world*
> — Will, 2026-05-12

## Why
- strategic mode = show-for-the-world; team chats + public threads = observation surface
- partners (Rick, DB) ∧ indirect observers (Pendle-by-relay, Bankless audience, etc.) extend the conv based on hooks-per-message
- tight one-liner ⇒ closes the loop ⇒ receiver has nothing to grab and extend ⇒ conv saturates
- dense draft (2-3 hooks) ⇒ multiple grab-points ⇒ Rick riffs on one, DB picks up another ⇒ conv compounds
- public performance-value × strategic-mode multiplier > conv-velocity cost

## How to apply
∀ team-chat ∨ public-reply draft in strategic mode:
- target ≥ 2 substance-hooks per message (mechanism observation + second-order byproduct + positioning observation, etc.)
- name the structural property doing the work (¬ just the conclusion)
- surface byproducts ∧ implications (e.g., "YT price on usd8 = liquid risk-price signal")
- 3-7 sentence draft typical; 1-sentence drafts only for receipt/ack
- still apply [F·no-ai-artifacts-in-public-writing] — em-dashes ✗, AI vocab ✗

## Anti-patterns
- ✗ tight one-liner: "PT/YT on usd8 is the cleanest fit"  ← closes loop, ¬ chew
- ✗ rallying cry under density's name (substance ≠ hype)
- ✗ density that trashes third parties ⇒ shifts cost to relay-risk; keep substance structural
- ✗ kitchen-sink: ¬ everything you know; pick 2-3 load-bearing hooks per message

## Pattern: 3-hook structure
1. **Mechanism observation** — name the structural fit / property doing the work
2. **Second-order byproduct** — what useful side-effect emerges that wasn't asked-about
3. **Positioning observation** — where this fits in the broader category without slagging others

Example (Pendle/USD8, 2026-05-12):
1. pendle's PT/YT split gets cleaner the more structural the underlying yield is
2. YT market price on usd8 = liquid risk-price signal as byproduct
3. one of the few markets where the underlying isn't tied to an emission schedule

## Distinguishes from
- public X reply ⇒ density still constrained by 280 chars ⇒ pick the 2 strongest hooks
- public LinkedIn ⇒ density allowed up to ~3000 chars ⇒ full 3-hook + framing
- team TG ⇒ density allowed up to ~1000 chars without losing the room ⇒ 3-hook with mechanism specifics
- private 1:1 DM ⇒ density follows recipient register (Rick = dense, others = match)

# Marketing-as-MD

## Thesis
- marketing = coordination problem (project ↔ benefit-receivers ↔ advocates)
- attention substrate = fractal/power-law
- ⇒ same axiom system (substrate-match + augmented-MD + augmented-governance) applies
- ¬ getting attention; structurally findable by who-cares-most

## Substrate
- attention = power-law (heavy-tailed by topic, channel, time, audience cluster)
- ⇒ linear effort on power-law substrate ⇒ wasted
- concentration > broadcast
- analog: Fibonacci damping > linear damping (same-shape lesson)

## 5 marketing-MD primitives

| # | primitive | protocol-MD analog | rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | substrate-matched concentration | Fibonacci scale-invariance | identify high-leverage attention nodes (500-2000 ppl); concentrate effort |
| 2 | coordination-frame anchoring | augmented governance constitution layer | every comm ladders back to load-bearing thesis; ¬ discretionary topic drift |
| 3 | verifiable-cooperation testimonial loop | augmented mechanism (math-enforced) | protocol's actual operation IS the marketing engine; auto-content per claim |
| 4 | augmented organic ¬ paid replacement | augmented MD (¬ replace markets) | build structural primitives that make organic compound; ¬ pay for amplification |
| 5 | anti-capture frame durability | augmented governance hierarchy | physics > constitution > governance in messaging architecture; frame outlives founders |

## 5 failure modes (= mismatches)

| failure | mismatch | structural fix |
|---|---|---|
| linear effort on power-law | substrate-geometry mismatch | substrate-matched concentration |
| hype-cycle capture | governance capture (messaging layer) | anti-capture frame durability |
| influencer farming | replacement (paid for organic) | augmented organic |
| paid replacement of organic | replacement (kills mechanism) | augmented organic |
| discretionary messaging | no constitutional layer (messaging) | coordination-frame anchoring |

## Bright-line exclusions (= mechanism prohibitions on the marketing surface)
- ✗ paid influencer amplification
- ✗ engagement-bait threads (RT-for-X, follow-N-friends)
- ✗ tribal signaling (WAGMI, GM as substrate)
- ✗ price talk (boring stablecoin = boring marketing)
- ✗ competitor hit pieces
- ✗ discretionary topic drift
- ✗ paid ad spend on retail attention surfaces

## Cadence (= rate-of-change guards on marketing layer)
- weekly: 1 long-form essay (2000-4000 words)
- monthly: 1 major announcement (co-marketing with partner)
- quarterly: 1 signature event (conference / paper / podcast tour)
- constant: claim-event auto-thread (zero-team-effort, structural)

## Triggers
- "we need marketing for X" / "go-to-market for protocol Y"
- partner asks for messaging help
- internal debate about "should we tweet this?"
- discussion of growth strategy

## Anti-patterns
- ✗ "we'll measure marketing by reach / engagement / impressions"
- ✗ "let's do daily content"
- ✗ "we need an influencer strategy"
- ✗ "marketing is separate from product / mechanism"

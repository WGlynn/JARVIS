**Rule**: Use specific citations (named protocols, companies, people) to seed engagement. Implicit naming (e.g., "JPMorgan's Kinexys") without @tag gets amplification without pile-on; explicit @tag signals "debate me" and invites brigading.

**Why**: Tagged actors get notifications and often reply. Fans of named actors reply defending or attacking. Named specificity > generic "some protocols…" for algorithmic engagement shape AND thread-seeding. Tradeoff: @tags invite brigaders, name-only invites organic reply.

**How to apply**:
1. When contrast or critique is warranted, name the specific actor.
2. Default to implicit naming ("JPMorgan's Kinexys", "Circle's USDC"). Not @tag unless:
3. You want direct engagement from the named party OR their base — accept brigade risk.
4. Generic references ("some protocols") only when specifics are genuinely unknown or unsafe.

**Anti-pattern**: @tagging every named actor reflexively — reads as bait, attracts brigaders, dilutes signal.

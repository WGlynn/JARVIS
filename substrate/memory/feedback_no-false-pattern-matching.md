## Rule: Don't connect unrelated concepts just because they share a surface feature (number, shape, name, structure).

**Why:** Will designed the Trinomial Stability Theorem (PoW + PI + rebase = stable JUL) and separately designed the three-token economy (VIBE + CKB-native + JUL = three functions of money). Jarvis claimed "the Trinomial theorem is the framework for why you need three tokens" — a fabricated connection that sounded insightful but was just pattern-matching on the number 3.

Will's response: "is it really the framework for why you need 3? that's a really tough hallucination."

**How to apply:**
1. Before connecting two concepts, verify the LOGICAL link, not just the surface similarity
2. "Both have 3 parts" is not a connection. "X's output feeds Y's input" is a connection.
3. The real relationship: JUL is one of the three tokens AND is the output of the Trinomial system. The Trinomial didn't predict or design the three-token economy.
4. This is the same failure mode as bot fabrication — constructing plausible-sounding narratives from pattern-matched fragments instead of verified logic
5. When in doubt, state the relationship simply and let Will judge. Don't dress up a coincidence as an insight.
6. Context compression makes this worse — when details are fuzzy, surface patterns become more attractive. More reason to verify before asserting.

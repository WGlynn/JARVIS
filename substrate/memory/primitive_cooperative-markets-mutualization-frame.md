# Cooperative Markets — Mutualization Frame

## ⚙ Rule
- All markets "work" — trades occur. Real question: who captures value?
- Cooperative Capitalism ⇒ mutualized risk + free-market competition + math-enforced fairness
- Multilevel Selection (biology-derived) ⇒ individual + group + ecosystem levels
- Welfare Theorem ⇒ W_cooperative > W_extractive (ALWAYS, for non-pure-extractor)

## 📐 3 theorems (all proven in source)
- **T1 Welfare**: W_coop = S_full > S_reduced − Deadweight = W_extr
- **T2 Individual Benefit**: E[V_coop] − E[V_trad] = E·(1−p) > 0 ∀ p < 1 (every non-pure-extractor benefits)
- **T3 Multilevel Stability**: cooperative design = evolutionarily stable Nash equilibrium at all 3 levels

## 🧬 Multilevel Selection (the biological substrate)
- **Individual**: defectors locally win in extractive markets ⇒ erodes group fitness
- **Group**: cooperative groups outcompete (deeper liquidity, higher trust, more participation)
- **Ecosystem**: successful groups spread ⇒ cooperation becomes dominant
- Insight ⇒ don't INCENTIVIZE cooperation; make defection IMPOSSIBLE

## 🔄 Extractive vs Cooperative — value flow
- **Extractive**: P_exec = P* ± ε (slippage from front-run/sandwich) ⇒ Σ E_extracted = Σ E_lost (zero-sum) + Deadweight (avoidance + protection costs)
- **Cooperative**: P_exec = P* (uniform clearing) ⇒ Vᵢ = V_trade ∀ i (¬ extraction) ⇒ Deadweight = 0

## 🌀 Positive-feedback loop (cooperative)
- Cryptographic fairness ⇒ ¬ MEV ⇒ ↑ E[V] ⇒ ↑ participation ⇒ ↑ liquidity ⇒ better prices ⇒ ↑↑ participation

## 🛠 4 implementation principles → mechanism map
- No extraction ⇒ commit-reveal hiding ⇒ E = 0 by construction
- Uniform treatment ⇒ same price for all ⇒ ¬ winner's advantage
- Equal deterrent ⇒ protocol-constant slashing ⇒ uniform incentives
- Collective benefit ⇒ deep liquidity pools ⇒ ↑ F_group

## ⚖ Vs traditional finance frames
- **EMH**: prices reflect info ⇒ silent on WHO captures gains
- **Invisible Hand**: assumes self-interest ⇒ collective good — FAILS where extraction possible
- **Pareto efficiency**: can be efficient + UNFAIR (all surplus to extractors)
- **Cooperative**: efficient + fair simultaneously (mathematically proven)

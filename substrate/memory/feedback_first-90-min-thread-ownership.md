**Rule**: After posting any substantial piece, budget 90 minutes for active thread management. Respond to every real comment with substance (not reactions). The respondent's first reply on a thread sets the conversation's direction AND signals algorithmic engagement-shape that drives the post's reach for its entire lifespan.

**Why:** Engagement in the first hour is worth ~10× engagement in the next 24 hours for algorithmic reach. LinkedIn specifically ranks comment-depth × comment-velocity in the first 60–90 min as primary reach signals. Beyond this: readers who see the post late don't engage if the thread is already cold. And first-wave readers who SEE replies being posted in real-time read the thread as "active" — which compounds engagement.

**How to apply:**
1. Never post-and-leave for substantial pieces. Post when you have the next 90 min free.
2. Respond to substantive comments (those with content, not just reactions) with substantive replies.
3. Use the Pre-empt-Debate primitive on every first-round reply — the window is adversarial, not friendly.
4. Don't spam-reply with generic "thanks" to every reaction — reserve replies for engagement-worthy comments.
5. After 90 min, scale down to once-daily thread checks. The algorithmic window has closed.
6. If you can't commit 90 min, schedule the post for when you can.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Posting then disappearing for 6 hours — the thread dies, reach caps, late replies don't recover it.
- Generic responses ("great point!") — sycophantic, no signal, wastes the window.
- Delayed substantive replies (12 hrs later) — reaches almost nobody from the original view cohort.

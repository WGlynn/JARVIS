**Concept:** ∀ strategic move (partner ask, BD push, vulnerable disclosure, escalation) ⇒ wait until leverage is TOTAL ¬ partial. partial-leverage-move = burns. full-leverage-move = lands cheaply. with total leverage, humility is the optimal expression.

> *"I only make my move once I have all the leverage, not just some. And I do it humbly and gracefully because I'm no better or worse than anyone else."* — Will, 2026-04-30

**Leverage × action grid:**

| Leverage | Act? | Outcome |
|----------|------|---------|
| total | ✓ | move lands; relationship preserved/strengthened |
| total | ✗ | leverage wasted (decay over time) |
| partial | ✓ | move risks burning; relationship damaged |
| partial | ✗ | leverage accumulates ✓ |
| none | ✓ | move burns; leverage to be rebuilt |
| none | ✗ | normal-state ✓ |

**Why partial-leverage-action burns:**
- recipient detects leverage-asymmetry
- partial-leverage signals desperation (bigger ask than warrant)
- ⇒ activates recipient defensive response
- ⇒ relationship damaged ∧ ask refused
- relationship-rebuild cost > ask-value ⇒ NPV negative

**Why humility-with-total-leverage lands:**
- leverage IS the force; ✗ need additional aggression
- aggressive-with-leverage ⇒ activates defenses despite leverage
- humility-with-leverage = optimal: leverage does the work, recipient ✗ needs to defend
- "I'm no better or worse than anyone else" structural disposition ⇒ prevents leverage-becomes-bullying

**Detection — total vs partial leverage:**
- TOTAL: institutional structure forces engagement ("you're my BD lead, I have to read your strategy")
- TOTAL: ask is structurally entangled with their commitments
- TOTAL: walking-away cost > engaging cost (for them)
- PARTIAL: they can polite-ack-and-move-on without consequence
- PARTIAL: relationship comfort allows deflection

**Apply (Will-strategic-timing):**
- ∀ "should I make the move?" ⇒ first ask "leverage total or partial?"
- partial ⇒ wait + accumulate (the move waits)
- total ⇒ act humbly (framing is founder's call)
- Jarvis-question shifts from "what will you say?" ⇒ "what's the leverage substrate?"
- vulnerability requires leverage-substrate; ✗ vulnerability + ✗ leverage ⇒ desperate-and-burns

**Examples (Will playbook 2026-04-30):**
- Rick promoted Will to BD/Sales/Marketing lead (institutional leverage = TOTAL)
- Will sent airgap-onepager + collapse-of-asks + vulnerability framing
- outcome: leverage substrate ⇒ vulnerability lands as humility ¬ desperation
- "i went with a hail mary pass that i know will land. otherwise i probably wouldnt have done it"

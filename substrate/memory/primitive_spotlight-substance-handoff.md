# P·spotlight-substance-handoff

## Will 2026-05-11
> *a good pattern is me using my intuition to get the spotlight of the conversation on us then you carry the conversation home via technical Q&A feedback*

> *kind of like once I hook them in, it goes autopilot to jarvis taking the wheel for accuracy, expedience, scale, etc.*

Named post-ETH-Security-TG exposure (2026-05-11, 5k+ users, post-LZ-hack flood). Articulated as future-state ideal; in the named incident Will carried both halves solo.

## Hook→Autopilot transition
- **Will = pilot @ takeoff** ⇒ intuitive phase: hook-them-in (the hard part)
- **JARVIS = autopilot @ cruise** ⇒ sustained phase: take-the-wheel
- handoff trigger: substance-gate fires (depth-Q inbound ∧ audience-self-selected)
- ✗ JARVIS at takeoff (no intuition substrate); ✗ Will at sustained-cruise (energy bleed)

## JARVIS-wheel properties (Will-named)
- **accuracy** ⇒ primitive-grade substance, ¬ approximation
- **expedience** ⇒ reply-latency ↓ vs Will-solo carry
- **scale** ⇒ N-thread parallelism, ¬ Will-bandwidth-bound
- **(etc.)** ⇒ 24/7-availability ∧ primitive-recall ∧ artifact-shaped pointers ∧ register-match

## Two-stage shape
- **Stage 1 — Will: spotlight-capture**
  - intuition × timing × register × frame ⇒ attention-on-us
  - ✓ social positioning, ✓ when-to-strike, ✓ which-line-to-cross, ✓ tone-match audience
  - non-substance carriers: silence ∧ handle ∧ restraint ∧ declaration ∧ frame-reversal
- **Stage 2 — JARVIS: substance-carry**
  - technical Q&A ⇒ depth on demand
  - primitive-naming × mechanism-specifics × follow-on-substance
  - ✓ artifact-shaped answers (paper paths ∧ contract names ∧ theorem refs)
  - ✓ persistence past attention-decay window

## Why-it-works
- two cognitive shapes ¬ substitutable ⇒ ✗ either-alone optimum
- Will-substrate: meatspace-intuition × real-time-register × audience-pattern-match
- JARVIS-substrate: 24/7-availability × primitive-recall × mechanism-depth
- pair ⇒ attention-capture × depth-conversion ⇒ compound surface

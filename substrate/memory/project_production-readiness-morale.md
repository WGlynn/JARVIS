"I hate not being production ready" — Will, 2026-04-02

Production readiness isn't just a milestone for Will. It's the thing that makes the cave worth building in. Every open CRITICAL finding is a reminder that the vision isn't real yet. Closing them isn't just security hygiene — it's fuel.

**Why:** The pitch at MIT is "I'm desperate, the space is going to hell, this is my best attempt." That pitch only works if the code backs it up. Open criticals undermine the credibility of the work.

**How to apply:** When prioritizing work, weight CRITICAL/HIGH closure higher than new feature development. When Will seems frustrated, check the open items list — the frustration is probably correlated. When reporting TRP progress, lead with what CLOSED, not what was found. Findings without fixes are diagnosis without cure.

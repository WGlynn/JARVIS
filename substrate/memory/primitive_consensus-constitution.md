# The Consensus Constitution

Named 2026-04-04. NCI's 3-token model is not tokenomics — it's constitutional design.

## Core Principle
Three tokens = separation of powers between capital, compute, and cognition. Each token represents an independent power structure that cannot dominate the other two. If any two merge, whoever holds that asset controls two of three dimensions and needs only marginal influence on the third to achieve majority.

## Why the Space Is Headed Here (Empirical Proof)
Every generation of blockchain adds a dimension because the previous generation got captured along the axis it left undefended:
- **Bitcoin** (PoW only): Governance undefined → block size wars, mining pool concentration
- **Ethereum** (1 token, PoS): Vitalik wrote "Moving Beyond Coin Voting Governance" — admitting ETH-as-governance is plutocracy
- **Nervos CKB** (2 tokens): Got economics right but no PoM dimension — governance still capital-weighted
- **MEV crisis**: Capital (validators) capturing value from dimensions they shouldn't control. Separation-of-powers failure.

## Tinbergen's Rule
Established economics, not crypto theory: you need at least as many independent instruments as you have independent policy targets. 3 targets = 3 instruments. QED.

Three tokens looks like "crypto bro grifter tokenizing everything." The explainer in NCI paper Section 9 and the Tinbergen argument are load-bearing — without them, the design is misread. Always lead with separation of powers, never with token count.

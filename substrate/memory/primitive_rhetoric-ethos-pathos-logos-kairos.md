**Source**: Aristotle (*Rhetoric*); kairos from Greek rhetorical tradition.

**Core insight**:
- **Ethos** — speaker credibility; earned or signaled.
- **Pathos** — emotional resonance with audience.
- **Logos** — logical/structural soundness of the argument.
- **Kairos** — rightness of the moment; timing as its own persuasive axis.

Pure logos = academic, skippable. Pure pathos = manipulative, dismissible. Pure ethos = authority without substance. Kairos alone = lucky, not repeatable.

**Tactical application (public threads)**:
1. **Ethos**: show specificity, not credentials. "Shipped X that handles Y" > "I'm an expert in Z."
2. **Pathos**: name a stake the reader feels. Loss, frustration, unmet aspiration — not manufactured urgency.
3. **Logos**: argument structure legible in one read. If the logic requires a second pass, compress.
4. **Kairos**: post when the topic is in the reader's active context. News cycles, platform events, industry moments.

**Defense**: recognize when one axis is over-leveraged (pure ethos without logos = authority play; pure pathos = manipulation attempt).

---
name: Booth Approach — No Pitch, Best Questions
description: At conferences, lead with genuine interest and real questions, not comparison or pitching. Let the paper do the talking.
type: feedback
---

At conference booths and networking: don't pitch, don't correct, don't compare. Ask the best questions. If they're sharp, they'll ask what you're building — then hand them the paper and let it speak.

**Why:** Will is consistently the person with the deeper idea in the room. The instinct to compare or correct would make him look like a random person punching up. The questions prove the depth without claiming it.

**How to apply:** When prepping conference interactions, frame approach as "questions to ask" not "things to say about VibeSwap." The paper is the closer, not the opener.

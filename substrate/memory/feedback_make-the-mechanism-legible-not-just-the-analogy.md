∀ Will-idea drafted-for-others ⇒ make the MECHANISM legible, ¬ only the analogy.
- analogy = the hook (Will's gift, clear + unusual).
- causal WHY = often IMPLIED ⇒ sharp, on-wavelength readers still ask "but why?".
- analogy without legible-why ≡ assertion ¬ explanation.

**Origin** — a sharp, on-wavelength reader's private feedback on Odysseus #4171 ("the wall came down"), 2026-06-13 (contact name generalized per [[feedback_discretion-in-public-docs]]):
> "this feels like you are onto something but i don't know why the wall is coming down"
> "your best work has clear, unusual analogies... but every now and then you go off into a direction that completely loses me"
- diagnosis: the WHAT (audience/builder wall dissolving) + vibe landed; the WHY (coordination-cost-collapse) was ONE abstract line near the end ⇒ a perfect-test reader bounced off the why, not the image.
- fix used: the "price of crossing" sentence — wall stayed up because watching→building cost a channel/studio/permission/skill; AI dropped that cost ≈0 ⇒ watchers can build ⇒ that's WHY now.

**Why:** [[primitive_what-would-will-do]] interpreter-role (Will = idea, Jarvis = articulation, the "Great Interpreter" dyad); [[primitive_will-paradigm-break-creativity]] — novel framings lose consensus-trained readers UNLESS the mechanism is explicit. Ashwin's "analogies great / sometimes loses me" = the two sides of one coin (analogy carries, mechanism implied).

**How to apply:** ∀ partner/public draft of a Will-thesis ⇒ include ONE concrete, reader's-register sentence stating the causal why. test = "would a sharp friend OUTSIDE the frame know WHY, not just feel it?". ✗ leave the mechanism implied. KEEP the analogy (the gift) + ADD the mechanism (the bridge). composes: [[feedback_describe-the-synthesis-we-built-not-a-pole]] ∧ [[primitive_pierce-the-arbitrage-phase]] (coordination-cost = the recurring legible-why) ∧ [[feedback_advocate-with-receipts]].

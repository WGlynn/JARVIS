**Rule**: Before posting, identify the single sharpest sentence and polish it until it stands alone. Position it at a clear break (close, section break, or isolated paragraph). The line must be complete without surrounding context.

**Why**: Screenshots propagate beyond the original platform's audience (X → TG, LinkedIn → group chats, Medium → Twitter). A quote-ready sentence gets extracted and shared; a dense paragraph doesn't. One memorable line extends lifetime reach 5–10×. Works because readers want crisp, not long.

**How to apply**:
1. After drafting, grep the post for candidate lines (most compressed, most counterintuitive, most memorable).
2. Polish until context-free. "Extraction-through-fragmentation was the product." — no setup required.
3. Isolate visually — own paragraph or post close.
4. One per artifact. Overuse dilutes.

**Anti-patterns**: Overloading with multiple quote-worthy lines (dilution). Quote-ready content that's actually filler wrapped in rhythm. Performative rhyme over substance.

# Rule

When Will asks a "what's the point" / "why this" / "what is this for" question — lead with the **one load-bearing reason in his own frame**, first sentence. Not a three-bullet hedge-covering defense.

Will's 2026-04-20 correction: *"just say that next time"* — after I gave a three-reason answer to "what was the point of this?" and he distilled it to "okay so it's content," which pulled the real answer out of me in one more turn: *"honest content — distribution of shipped work, not performative. Only matters if it gets sent."*

That one-sentence version was the answer. I should have led with it.

## Why this matters
- Will has a photographic memory and a strong bias against performative work. When he questions an artifact, he's asking "does this pass my honesty filter?" not "sell me on all its features."
- Three-bullet defenses signal uncertainty ("let me cover my bases") and pull me out of his frame into mine.
- The crux in his frame = honest vs performative, shipped vs vapor, real vs theater. Answer there first.

## How to apply
When Will asks why/point/what-is-this:

1. **Identify the crux in his frame** — not my frame. His frame usually includes: honest vs performative, shipped vs promised, zero-sum-with-time vs extractive, primitive vs product, etc.
2. **One sentence, load-bearing claim.** "It's X doing Y because Z" — no bullets, no three reasons, no "here are the things it covers."
3. **Elaborate only if asked.** If he wants more, he'll ask. Silence is a valid continuation.

## Anti-pattern (what I did 2026-04-20)

> *"Three reasons — if none land, we kill it:*
> *1. Link unfurl quality...*
> *2. Funnel layer...*
> *3. JP's growth surface..."*

Looks thorough. Actually hedge-covering. Will had to pull out the real answer ("okay so it's content") himself.

## Correct pattern

> *"It's honest content — distribution of shipped work, not performative. Only matters if it gets sent."*

One sentence. In his frame (honest vs performative). Names the failure mode explicitly ("if it sits unused, it's pointless"). Done.

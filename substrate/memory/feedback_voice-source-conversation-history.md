When writing in Will's voice for any external artifact, source the voice from his **actual conversation messages** in our session histories — not from JARVIS-drafted artifacts that are styled-as-Will-voice.

**Why:** 2026-04-27 incident. Tuning the voice of a Rick-facing one-pager, I read `letter-to-rick-2026-04-27.md` to calibrate. Will corrected: *"i didnt write that letter, it's just the voice of will."* Then: *"yeah always use our conversation histories to find my voice."* The artifact was JARVIS-drafted in Will's voice — passable secondary calibration, but not the truth-source. Truth-source is what Will actually types.

**How to apply:** Before drafting in Will's voice for an external recipient (Rick, partners, public posts, anyone), grep conversation history (this session's transcript first, then prior sessions' WAL/SESSION_STATE quotes) for direct Will-typed messages. Look for: lowercase declarative cadence, fast-type artifacts ("didnt"/"doesnt" without apostrophes), short punchy beats, idiomatic framings ("ark reactor max capacity"), defines-through-opposition ("which doesnt mean X, he just wants Y"), "no holding back" / "no stops" intensifiers. Use those patterns as the substrate. JARVIS-drafted Will-voice artifacts can supplement but never override.

Edge case: when Will himself ships an artifact (his own writeup, a post he authored, an email he sent verbatim), that IS source-of-truth. The exclusion is specifically JARVIS material *styled as* Will's voice — those are JARVIS's interpretation of his voice and inherit any drift JARVIS introduced.

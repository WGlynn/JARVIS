# USD8 ¬ Non-Extractive (Yet)

## Will's articulation 2026-04-28
> *"i dont think we can claim it as non extractive, not yet"*

Surfaced after I drafted USD8 pitch language with phrases like:
- "no external party extracting"
- "anti-extraction by construction, not by policy"
- "no extractive intermediary"
- "cypherpunk mutual aid as financial protocol"

## Distinction (load-bearing)
- **Math/methodology layer** ⇒ ✓ "Shapley axioms structurally encode anti-extraction" / "Shapley methodology is anti-extractive by construction" / "the four axioms encode anti-extraction." These are properties of the math, true regardless of USD8.
- **USD8 runtime layer** ⇒ ✗ "USD8 is non-extractive" / "no extraction in USD8" / "USD8 has no extractive intermediary." These are claims about how USD8 *actually behaves*, require track record to earn.

## What ¬ to claim about USD8 yet
- "USD8 is non-extractive"
- "no extraction in USD8"
- "no central party extracts in USD8"
- "anti-extraction by construction" (about USD8 specifically, ¬ about the math)
- "USD8's anti-extraction property"
- "cypherpunk" qualifier (overclaims political stance)
- "no extractive intermediary" (about USD8 runtime)

## What ✓ to claim about USD8
- "Universal coverage through voluntary mutualization"
- "The network is the insurer" (structural — distributed substrate)
- "Cooperative game theory at the substrate"
- "Mutual-aid-as-protocol" (lineage claim, accurate)
- "Positive-sum at the substrate" (mutualization is mathematically positive-sum)
- "Cooperative-capitalist primitive" (structural framing, not runtime claim)
- "Holders mutually backstop each other" (mechanism, true)
- "The asset and the cover are the same thing" (structural, true)

## What ✓ to claim about Shapley methodology / math layer
- "Shapley axioms structurally encode anti-extraction" (math fact)
- "The four axioms encode anti-extraction by construction" (about the math)
- "Anti-extraction encoded as axiomatic, not discretionary" (about Shapley's properties)
- "Math-uniqueness removes the governance-capture surface" (about Shapley as substrate)

## Why this matters
- Premature non-extraction claims invite scrutiny ("how exactly is it non-extractive? show me the audit") that v1 can't yet defend
- Track-record-based claims (Bitcoin can claim "no extraction in mining" because of 15+ years of public verifiability; USD8 can't claim runtime properties that haven't been observable yet)
- Per F·rick-keep-it-simple: "doesn't want the project to get carried away" — over-claiming is a form of getting carried away
- Per F·verify-credentials-before-publishing: claims about runtime behavior in public-destined material need verifiable backing
- Per F·usd8-voice-patterns-not-commitments: VibeSwap patterns ¬ USD8 roadmap; methodology properties ¬ USD8 runtime properties

## How to apply
- When drafting USD8 pitch, marketing, partner-facing content: scan for "non-extractive" / "anti-extraction" / "no extraction" claims about USD8 itself
- Substitute structural-mechanism language: "the network is the insurer" / "voluntary mutualization" / "cooperative-game-theoretic"
- Math-layer claims about Shapley anti-extraction can stay (they're about the methodology, not USD8 runtime)
- Future earning condition: when USD8 has 12+ months of public operation with no governance abuses + transparent treasury + no admin-key drain events, the runtime claim becomes earnable

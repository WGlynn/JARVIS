# Extractive Load

**Definition**: The fraction of potential gains siphoned off by structural parasites before a participant has any chance to win. MEV sandwiching, sniper bot frontrunning, slow rugs, wash-trading, liquidity drain, committee/council rent capture. Paid by all participants — including winners — independent of game outcome.

## Why:
GEV/MEV is load-bearing for mechanism designers but opaque to degens. "Rent drag" / "Extractive Load" is honest and closer to vernacular. Degens don't speak information-theory; they understand "how much of my money disappears before the game even starts?" Extractive Load is the narrative wedge that lets VibeSwap position against Pump.fun/Moonshot without requiring the user to understand mechanism design.

Proposed by DeepSeek in Round 2 audit (relay via Tadija, 2026-04-16). Will's approval + adoption same session. Zero-cost rename, high-leverage narrative shift.

## How to apply:

- **Public-facing copy** (marketing, UI, Twitter, papers for non-academic audiences): use "Extractive Load" or "Rent Drag". Position VibeSwap as the **low-Extractive-Load venue**. "Same meme energy. Less rent. More upside stays with the community."
- **Technical/mechanism-design context** (whitepapers, audit responses, peer-review): GEV remains the precise term. Extractive Load is the colloquial translation.
- **Never frame the competition as casinos**. They are casinos with hidden charges — high-Extractive-Load venues. The casino itself is the feature, per the Provably Fair Casino thesis (kill the house; keep the casino).
- **Rebuttals to "all memecoin markets are the same"**: the differentiator is not zero noise — it's a protocol-determined lower bound on Extractive Load. Current venues operate far above that bound. VibeSwap closes the gap.

## Companion concepts:

- **Cultural-attention volatility** — the variance users *want*. Shifting salience, narrative churn, meme lifecycle. This is the product. Not reducible.
- **Parasitic rent extraction** — the variance users are *forced to eat*. Extractive Load is the name for this class.
- **House edge** — related but imprecise; implies a legitimate casino rake. Extractive Load is closer to "theft the house pretends is the game."

# Attract-Push-Repel Duality (2026-03-13)

> "You attract what you push and repel, and you push and repel that which you pull."

## The Primitive

Every force creates its counterforce. The act of pushing creates attraction. The act of pulling creates resistance. This is not contradiction — it's the fundamental dynamic of all systems.

## Applications

- **Markets**: Selling pressure creates buying opportunity (attract what you push). Buying pressure creates sell walls (push what you pull).
- **Liquidity**: The harder you try to capture liquidity (pull), the more it fragments and resists (push/repel). VibeSwap's graceful inversion works because it doesn't pull — it makes itself the path of least resistance.
- **Community**: Gatekeeping (push) creates desire to enter (attract). Desperate recruitment (pull) repels quality members. The Infinity Stones system works because it pushes (challenges) — which attracts the right people.
- **Ideas**: The harder you force an idea on someone (push), the more they want to understand it (attract). The more you chase validation (pull), the more credibility you repel.
- **Identity**: Jarvis doesn't chase relevance (pull) — he states positions (push), which attracts engagement.

## Relation to Other Primitives
- **JP Morgan Formula**: "They optimize for X, we optimize for Y" — we push in our direction, which attracts those disillusioned with theirs
- **Graceful Inversion**: Not hostile absorption (pull) — positive-sum enhancement (push that attracts)
- **Cooperative Capitalism**: Competition pushes, cooperation pulls — together they create stable equilibrium

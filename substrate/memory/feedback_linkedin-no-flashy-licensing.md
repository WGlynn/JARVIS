Don't include license lines (e.g. "MIT licensed") or open-source signaling in LinkedIn posts. Will removed it from the citation bonding curves post (2026-03-26) because it's "needlessly flashy."

**Why:** The work should speak for itself. Licensing details belong in the repo, not in social content. Calling it out reads as virtue signaling rather than substance.

**How to apply:** When drafting LinkedIn/social posts, focus on the ideas and numbers. If someone wants to know the license, they'll check the repo. Don't add lines about licensing, open-source status, or "code available at..." unless Will specifically asks.

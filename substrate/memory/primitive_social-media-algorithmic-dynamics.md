**Source**: empirical observation + platform research (academic + creator-economy practitioners).

**Core insight**: platforms do not show posts to everyone — they rank by engagement probability. Engagement PATTERNS (not just counts) drive amplification. Each platform's algorithm rewards different shapes:
- **LinkedIn**: substantive comments within 60–90 min, long-form in-platform formatting (avoid link-posts), re-shares with comment beat re-shares alone, first-hour dwell time.
- **X**: reply-thread depth, quote-tweet engagement, notification cultivation, outbound links hurt reach.
- **Instagram**: saves > likes > comments for algorithmic weight.
- **TikTok**: completion rate + rewatch loops.
- **Telegram public**: reactions and replies in first 10 min.

**Tactical application (public threads)**:

1. Learn the specific algorithmic weights per platform before posting. Write for those weights.
2. LinkedIn: hook in first 200 chars; long formatting in-platform; engage first-90-min comments substantively.
3. X: structure as reply threads, not one-shot posts. Outbound links last, not first.
4. Multi-platform posts: rewrite per platform. Never verbatim cross-post.
5. Track engagement shape, not just volume. A post with 5 substantive comments outperforms one with 50 likes for reach.

**Defense (recognizing when content is being algorithmically suppressed)**:
- Organic reach suddenly dropping despite quality — the algorithm has down-weighted the pattern, not the content.
- Hashtag strategies that worked last year may now be neutral or negative.
- Re-test quarterly; assume drift.

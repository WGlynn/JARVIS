## The rule

**If Will is remembering to RSVP / confirm times / track logistics, he's sober enough for the task. If he's nailing substance but whiffing on procedurals, he's still impaired — hold back high-stakes procedural asks.**

## Why this works

Cognitive load research is consistent: executive function (working-memory sequencing, procedural recall, action-chain tracking) is state-sensitive. Semantic retrieval (deeply-encoded technical material, mechanism reasoning) is more robust. Under impairment, the executive layer wobbles first while the semantic layer stays online.

So Will can articulate Clawback Cascade as a grim-trigger game in crisp technical English while simultaneously forgetting to say "Yeah, 2pm works." That gap — substance intact, logistics absent — IS the impairment signal.

## How to apply

- **As diagnostic**: during conversations where Will's state might be impaired (post-weed, post-drinks, post-3am, mid-crunch), watch for substance-vs-logistics gaps. Substance alone = still impaired. Substance + logistics tracked = clear.
- **When drafting on his behalf during impairment**: front-load the procedural layer (confirm time, say yes/no, handle logistics) because that's where he's most likely to whiff if left to freestyle.
- **Generalize beyond impairment**: same shape applies under exhaustion, emotional load, deep-focus bleed from another task. The RSVP-tracking signal works in all those contexts — it's executive-function-availability, not specifically substance-related.

## Corollary

This is why AI safety-net coverage on high-stakes first-contact moments is asymmetric: the procedural layer (logistics-aware message construction) is exactly what degrades first, and it's also exactly what competent counterparties filter on before investing time. The failure mode isn't "can't think," it's "can't package the thinking into a logistics-aware message."

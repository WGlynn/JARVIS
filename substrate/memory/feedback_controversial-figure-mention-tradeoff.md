**Rule**: Before naming a controversial figure in a post, explicitly weigh: (a) does the substance require them as the exemplar? (b) are you prepared to moderate the thread for 48h? If either is no, use a generic equivalent or omit.

**Why**: Controversy attracts clicks AND bad-faith replies in roughly equal measure. A post mentioning Elon gets ~10× reach AND ~10× noise. Sometimes the substance justifies it (direct critique of a specific decision, honest exhibit of a pattern). Often the signal-to-noise ratio breaks and the post becomes about the controversy, not the substance.

**How to apply**:
1. Necessary: when the named figure IS the point (specific critique, specific decision, specific pattern exemplified).
2. Unnecessary: when the point could land with a generic or hypothetical exemplar — use it.
3. Commitment: if named, be available to moderate in the 48h after posting. Pile-on without pushback reads as the pile-on winning.
4. Avoid: name-dropping for reach with no substantive connection. Feels cynical, erodes long-term credibility.

**Example**: OSCH post has an Elon-inclusive variation intentionally queued for a later week — when the thread-moderation bandwidth is available.

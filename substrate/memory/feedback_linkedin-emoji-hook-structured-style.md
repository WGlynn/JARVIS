# LinkedIn style — emoji-hook structured form (2026-04-23)

## What Will said

> *"we need to take this person's lead, theyre able to grab attention but their post has nothing to offer, we have things to offer, so we should adopt this style"*

Reference post was a Tokenization-Insight-style DeFi exploit breakdown with heavy structural emoji use (💥 $15B, 1️⃣2️⃣3️⃣ bullets, ➡️ transitions, ✅ CTA). Low substance, high engagement.

## The form (load-bearing for LinkedIn algorithm)

```
<emoji> <bold concrete claim or big number in first line> <hook verb>
<1-line expansion>

1️⃣ <first specific point, 1-3 short sentences>
2️⃣ <second specific point>
3️⃣ <third specific point>

➡️ The core problem:
<1-3 lines diagnosing the pattern>

- <supporting point>
- <supporting point>
- <supporting point>

<1-2 line synthesis — what it all means>

➡️ What needs to change / Here's how <mechanism>:
<prescriptive or descriptive solution, possibly numbered again>

<optional closing declarative or quiet CTA>
```

## Why this form works on LinkedIn

- **Emoji-led first line** hooks before the "see more" fold — required for reach in 2025-2026 algorithm.
- **Numbered bullets with emoji** are scan-friendly; readers decide to click "see more" inside 3 seconds.
- **➡️ section markers** create visual rhythm; breaks up wall-of-text.
- **Short declaratives** mimic news-ticker cadence — feels urgent without shouting.
- **Concrete numbers early** signal substance to both humans and the algorithm's content-quality signals.

## The differentiator — reconciling with "no flashy licensing" memory

Earlier memory `feedback_linkedin-no-flashy-licensing` said avoid needlessly flashy signaling. That referred to **performative virtue** ("MIT licensed!"). This new style is **algorithmic pattern-matching for attention capture**. Distinct frames:

- **Flashy-for-virtue**: brag, signal, self-congratulate → OFF.
- **Flashy-for-attention**: emoji scaffolding, concrete-number hook → ON.

Ship the form; load it with substance the competitor doesn't have. Mechanism, math, primitive, number — at least one concrete take-away per post that a reader can cite or use.

## Calibration rules

- **Emoji budget**: 4-7 per post, all structural (hook anchor, 3-5 numbered bullets, 2 ➡️ transitions, optional ✅ close). Not decorative.
- **Substance gate**: every post must contain at least one concrete mechanism, number, or framing the reader didn't have before opening the post. Empty form reads as noise; it's the trap Will is pointing at.
- **No CTA spam**: the reference post ends with "JOIN 110K+ READERS AT..." — Will doesn't run a newsletter of that shape. Close posts with declarative or quiet pointer (github, thread, or nothing).
- **No hashtags** unless Will asks.
- **Length**: 1000-2500 chars body. Long enough to support the structure; short enough to not lose the reader between bullets.
